# 🌸 Blush Flow - Cute Period & Cycle Tracker

**Blush Flow** is a cute, privacy-first, pastel-themed menstruation and cycle tracking app built specifically for mobile devices and iPhones. It helps you track your cycle, predict future period and ovulation windows, log intimacy, and receive phase-specific health encouragement!

---

## ✨ Features

- 📅 **Monthly Calendar View**: Clear visual indicators for logged period days, predicted period windows, intercourse activity, and peak ovulation days.
- 🩸 **Custom Icon Indicators**:
  - **Blood Drop**: Solid red for actual period flow, dashed pink border for predicted periods.
  - **Heart**: Red for unprotected intercourse, pastel purple for protected intercourse.
  - **Yellow Star**: Cute star indicator for estimated peak ovulation days.
- 💡 **Cycle-Synced Advice & Encouragement**: Dedicated **Tips** tab providing tailored care advice based on your current cycle phase.
- 🔄 **3-Month Onboarding**: Built-in historical setup to accurately calculate your personal average cycle length right from day one.
- 📊 **Cycle Insights & History**: View your average cycle length, average period duration, and upcoming projected dates at a glance.
- 🔒 **100% Private & Device-Local**: All data is saved directly on your phone using browser LocalStorage. No server required, no accounts to create, and zero tracking.

## 📱 How to Install on iPhone (No App Store Needed!)

You can install **Blush Flow** onto your iPhone Home Screen in less than a minute—completely free!

1. **Open the App Link**: Open the shared link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. **Tap Share**: Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom of the Safari screen (the square with an up arrow `[↑]`).
3. **Add to Home Screen**:
   - Scroll down the menu and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - Type **Blush Flow** as the title.
   - Tap **Add** in the top right corner.

✨ **Done!** The cute **Blush Flow** icon will now appear on your iPhone Home Screen, opening in full-screen native app mode without browser address bars!

## 🛠️ How to Host Your Own Copy (GitHub Pages)

Want to host your own instance of this app?

1. **Fork or Clone** this repository to your GitHub account.
2. Go to your repository **Settings** > **Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to `Deploy from a branch`.
4. Select the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
5. GitHub will provide your live site URL in 1–2 minutes!
